## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes an artisanal indie-hacker ethos with high-end digital product design (evoking modern craft benchmarks like ReadCV, Linear, and Cosmos). Created for ambitious founders, engineers, designers, and multidisciplinary creators, it projects taste, agency, and momentum.

The visual style is **warm editorial minimalism**:
- **Palette**: Tactile cream and bone paper surfaces replace sterile white or uninspired dark voids, grounding the experience in physical, bespoke quality.
- **Tone**: Decisive, inspiring, and technically competent. It respects builder autonomy by steering clear of generic corporate SaaS gloss.
- **Accents**: High-energy persimmon-coral drives interactive focus, counterbalanced by structured charcoal ink, crisp hairline borders, and monospaced technical telemetry.
- **Atmosphere**: Quiet, high-velocity workbench vibes where every interaction feels deliberate, tactile, and sharp.

## Colors

The color system anchors on a balanced dual-world palette: an organic paper ground paired with industrial charcoal typography and an intense, electric terracotta/persimmon engine.

- **Primary (`#E25537`)**: The vibrant catalyst. Applied strictly to primary actions, interactive highlights, active badges, and essential emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#191919`)**: Deep charcoal soot ink. Used for high-contrast headlines, crisp border structures, and secondary solid controls.
- **Tertiary (`#4682B4`)**: Steel slate blue, pulled from atmospheric sky accents, reserved for metadata tags, technical indicators, and secondary project states.
- **Neutral (`#F7F3E9`)**: Warm natural parchment canvas that shields users from high-glare eye fatigue while cultivating a tactile studio feel.

Surface hierarchy moves upward from base canvas (`#F7F3E9`) to elevated interactive cards (`#FFFFFF` with warm undertones) or muted inner insets (`#EFE9DC`).

## Typography

The typographic architecture creates an editorial dialogue between humanistic heritage and technical execution:

- **Display & Section Titles (Newsreader)**: Evokes literary prestige, creative vision, and craft. Italicized words within headlines provide rhythm and organic punch (e.g., *"build real products with real people"*).
- **Body & Product Interfaces (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Highly legible, neutral, and contemporary. It balances high-density information layouts, cards, and profile bios without visual clutter.
- **System Telemetry & Badges (Space Mono)**: Injects builder authenticity, uppercase technical readouts, project index numbers (`01`, `02`), dates, and role tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on a strict 12-column modular grid bounded by a maximum width of `1280px` for desktop screens, collapsing cleanly across device tiers:
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer canvas `margin: 3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, outer canvas `margin: 2rem`.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, outer canvas `margin: 1.25rem`.

Card structures adhere strictly to 4px/8px micro-increments. Component groups prefer explicit inner borders over large empty gaps, maintaining density suited for workspaces and builder hubs. Section divisions use hairline horizontal ruling lines (`1px solid rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.1)`) complemented by subtle grid-intersection coordinate marks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids heavy artificial box-shadows in favor of **structural containment and tonal layers**:

1. **Card Architecture**: Flat porcelain or crisp white fill framed by subtle hairlines (`1px solid rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.08)`).
2. **Elevated Overlays & Modals**: Soft tactile lift (`0 8px 30px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.04)`) with crisp containment borders.
3. **Hover Micro-states**: Hovered list items and action cards trigger an ambient lift of `-2px` Y-translation, coupled with a hairline border tint shift to `rgba(226, 85, 55, 0.4)`.
4. **Canvas Texture**: Faint micro-stipple or architectural dot-matrix lines on the `#F7F3E9` background to give tactile grounding without competing with live text.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is purposeful and differentiated by function:
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to invite immediate touch and click interaction.
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers**: Balanced rounded geometry (`0.5rem` / `8px` base, scaling to `1rem` / `16px` for hero showcases), mirroring physical paper notebooks and technical toolkits.
- **Form Inputs**: Soft rounded rectangles (`0.5rem` / `8px`) maintaining seamless alignment with standard component heights.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#E25537` background, white text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `12px 24px`. Micro-hover: subtle shift to `#CB4428` with smooth `150ms` easing and `-1px` vertical translation.
- **Secondary**: Charcoal `#191919` surface, white text, pill-shaped, crisp hover glow.
- **Outline / Ghost**: Hairline border (`1px solid rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.2)`), warm surface background, `#191919` text, transition to `#EFE9DC` on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped geometry with `Space Mono` typography at `11px`, uppercase, letter-spaced `0.06em`.
- **Role Chips** (e.g., `DEV`, `FOUNDER`, `DESIGN`): Bordered translucent fills (`rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.06)`), neutral charcoal text, paired with a miniature 6px indicator dot.
- **Status Badges**: `accent-orange-subtle` background with `#E25537` text for high-priority opportunities or hiring states.

### Cards
- Clean rectangular panels with `8px` or `12px` border radius, bordered with `1px solid rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.08)`.
- Cards feature numbered metadata tags in the top-right corner using `Space Mono` (e.g., `01`, `02`) in `#8B929A`.
- Header areas within cards maintain an editorial headline paired with concise sans-serif descriptions.

### Lists & Row Items
- Interactive rows delimited by clean horizontal dividers (`border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.08)`).
- Hover state: Background subtly shifts to warm ivory (`#FBF7EE`), moving the right-facing chevron or metadata label `4px` outward.

### Input Fields
- Generous touch targets (`44px` height), padded `12px 16px`, with `1px solid rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.15)` border on `#FFFFFF` base.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#E25537` with an ambient ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(226, 85, 55, 0.15)`.
- Labels set in `Space Mono` or semibold `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp, understated `4px` rounded squares (checkbox) or circles (radio), `18px` diameter.
- Checked state: Filled `#E25537` with crisp white iconography, no heavy drop-shadows.

### Profile & Collab Avatars
- Stacked overlapping circular avatars with `2px solid #F7F3E9` separator borders, followed by a pill badge indicating active team count.

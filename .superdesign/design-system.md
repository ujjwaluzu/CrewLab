# CrewLab design system

CrewLab is an India-first builder community. Its marketing site combines a dark editorial product system with warm paper surfaces elsewhere. For this explainer, preserve the CrewLab black-charcoal base, cream typography, and coral-red accent while replacing the visual metaphor completely. Avoid generic dashboard cards and stock 3D assets. Make the project, people, and joining flow feel tangible and connected.

- Colors: charcoal `#0d0d0c`, panel `#111110`, cream `#ece9e0`, muted gray `#aaa69d`, CrewLab coral `#f04a2e`.
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans / Space Grotesk; Newsreader for editorial display; Space Mono for technical labels.
- Shapes: hairline borders, geometric primitives, square panel edges, concise uppercase monospace labels.
- Interaction: keyboard-first HTML controls, explicit selected state, concise live explanation, motion that stops for reduced-motion users.
- The real CrewLab logo is `src/components/BrandMark.tsx`; preserve it anywhere the global site shell appears.
- Repo: Next.js App Router, React 19, TypeScript and plain CSS. `ogl` is already installed for WebGL.

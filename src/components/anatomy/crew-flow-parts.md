# CrewLab flow bench: concept and parts list

**Size:** Hero. A project brief travels on a guided carriage past five working stations; each station is the next real CrewLab workflow step, and the project card's position is the live selection.

## Assemblies (target: 100–125 visible solids and mechanisms)

- **Bench base (16):** four two-piece feet, main bed plate, inset bed seam, corner screws and rings, project-flow name plate, two etched rule rails, and short scale marks.
- **Guide and moving brief carriage (22):** two rails, four saddles, six hold-down bolts, carriage shoe, four roller discs, two axles, card cradle, upright card, two card clamps, four rivets, pointer lamp, lamp shade, spring guard, and a status dial.
- **Profile cabinet (15):** cabinet plinth and case, three drawer faces, six drawer pulls, skill-token rail, four skill discs, profile name slip, and cabinet fasteners.
- **Discovery optic (18):** lens pedestal, two supports, pivot, loupe bezel and glass, three nested rings, lens handle, finder shoe, pair of sample listing plates, skill filter wheel, and wheel detents.
- **Owner review press (18):** request tray, incoming ticket, guide rails, review platen, hinge knuckle, approval lever, lever grip, return spring, ink pad, approval stamp, two status pins, tray stops, and corner fasteners.
- **Crew worktable (17):** table top and apron, four legs, two clamp blocks, two screw handles, shared notes plate, three member pegs, two note clips, and cable eyelets.
- **Read-only repository reader (17):** reader base and hood, dark inspection window, two page rails, repository wafer, three branch tabs, issue register dial, two lock halves, lock pin, read-only shutter, and fasteners.
- **Structure and cable runs (12):** two back uprights, cross rail, two diagonal braces, five cable collars, status-wire segments, and terminal ferrules.

The etched plate carries the only in-object wording. Stage names, explanations, interaction hints, and live readout stay in HTML below or beside the drawing.
